# Jobs to Be Done: Candidate Framework

## Goal

Define candidate user jobs for research and product testing without presenting them as validated customer needs.

## Evidence status

The jobs below were derived from product capabilities and plausible scenarios. They have not yet been validated or prioritized through Narayana interviews, support data, search data or product analytics.

## Candidate jobs

| Situation | Candidate job | Potential Narayana capability | Important constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| A person wants separate contact details for work | Keep work communication separate from personal communication | Virtual number, eSIM or business number | Required calls, SMS, longevity and app compatibility must be confirmed |
| A person relocates while retaining accounts in another country | Maintain access to existing communication while adding local service | eSIM, international or virtual number | Keeping a number and receiving verification messages are separate capabilities |
| A person must share contact details with an unfamiliar party | Limit disclosure of a primary number | Virtual number or secondary line | This may reduce exposure, but does not prevent all spam or data leakage |
| A traveller needs connectivity after arrival | Activate mobile data without visiting a local shop | Travel eSIM | Many travel eSIMs are data-only |
| A person needs a number for an online account | Obtain an eligible number without using the primary line | Virtual or mobile number | Acceptance varies by service; compatibility must never be guaranteed without evidence |
| A person or business needs a local contact point abroad | Provide a reachable number in another country | International or virtual number | Local-presence, identity and regulatory requirements vary |
| A person manages several communication contexts | Keep contact identities operationally separate | Multiple numbers or subscriptions | Separation is not anonymity and may add complexity |
| A person or business makes international calls | Control cost and reliability across countries | SIP, calling or international number | Price and quality depend on destination and route |
| A business needs programmable communications | Integrate calling or numbering into an existing system | API, SIP or virtual numbers | Requires technical, regulatory and operational assessment |

## Research questions

- Which candidate jobs appear most often in support, search and purchase data?
- What triggers the job, and what outcome marks success?
- What alternatives do customers use today?
- Which capabilities are mandatory, and which are merely preferred?
- What causes failure: compatibility, activation, pricing, documentation or trust?
- Which words do customers use before they know the relevant product category?

## Current conclusion

This framework is a recruitment and interview guide. It does not support claims that users generally avoid telecom terminology, that the listed jobs are common, or that a specific Narayana product is the correct solution before its constraints are checked.
