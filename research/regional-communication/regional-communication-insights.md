# Regional Communication Hypotheses

## Purpose

Translate the evidence gaps in [Regional Communication Habits](regional-communication-habits.md) and the capabilities in [Digital Identity and Phone-Number Usage](digital-identity-phone-number-usage.md) into testable product questions.

## Evidence status

Every statement in this document is a hypothesis or proposed research direction. None should be presented as validated Narayana user behaviour.

## Hypothesis 1: users begin with a situation, not a product category

Possible user language includes “I need internet after landing” or “I need a separate number for work.” It is not yet known how frequently Narayana customers use this language or which situations are most common.

Test with search-query data, support-ticket coding, interviews and a scenario-led navigation experiment.

## Hypothesis 2: the best entry channel varies by country and task

A messenger used for personal communication is not necessarily preferred for purchase, support or access to sensitive account data.

Test channel preference separately for discovery, notifications, support, purchase and account management in each priority country.

## Hypothesis 3: migration and travel create multi-line needs

Relocation and travel may create a need to keep one subscription while adding another. This does not establish demand for a specific Narayana product.

Test which capabilities are required: data, SMS, calls, a persistent number, a particular country code or compatibility with a named service.

## Hypothesis 4: identity separation is a meaningful value proposition

Some users may separate work, personal, public and temporary contact details. The prevalence, willingness to pay and acceptable setup complexity are unknown.

Test with customer interviews and product analytics rather than assuming that every additional-number purchase is privacy-motivated.

## Hypothesis 5: scenario-led explanations improve product selection

Explaining eSIMs, virtual numbers and SIP through concrete situations may help non-specialists choose correctly.

Measure selection accuracy, purchase completion, refunds and compatibility-related support contacts against the current category-led experience.

## Current conclusion

The regional work supports a research plan, not a regional go-to-market decision. Narayana should prioritize countries using its own customer and acquisition data, then validate channel and use-case hypotheses within those markets.
