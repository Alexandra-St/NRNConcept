# Regional Communication Research

This research block separates observed communication habits from product interpretation.

## Documents

- [Regional Communication Habits](regional-communication-habits.md) — verified platform facts, unsupported regional claims removed, and the required research design.
- [Digital Identity and Phone-Number Usage](digital-identity-phone-number-usage.md) — verified platform and device capabilities separated from candidate use cases.
- [Regional Communication Hypotheses](regional-communication-insights.md) — testable product and channel hypotheses.

## Research flow

1. Use `regional-communication-habits.md` for the limits of current regional evidence and the proposed country-level method.
2. Use `digital-identity-phone-number-usage.md` for sourced phone-number and eSIM capabilities.
3. Use `regional-communication-insights.md` for hypotheses, not conclusions.
4. Keep future synthesis documents, such as **User Communication Behaviour**, linked to these source layers rather than repeating their content.
