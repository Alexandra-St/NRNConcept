# Competitor Communication Ecosystem

## Goal

Understand how competitors interact with users across different platforms and how users discover products, learn about them, receive support and build trust.

This document is a research framework. Its examples are candidate touchpoints, not findings about actual user behaviour. Verified results belong in the [Communication Channels Audit](communication-channels-audit.md).

For current verified findings, see [Communication Channels Audit](communication-channels-audit.md).

For the broader platform context behind messenger-based business interaction, see [Business Messaging Ecosystem](business-messaging-ecosystem.md).

---

## Website

Research:

- landing pages;
- product presentation;
- pricing;
- documentation;
- FAQ;
- blog;
- account creation;
- privacy policy;
- terms of service.

Observe:

- whether products can be explored without registration;
- whether pricing is transparent;
- whether technical documentation is publicly available;
- whether the website requires personal information before purchase.

## User Entry Points

Research where users discover the company; do not assume likelihood without acquisition evidence.

Use [User Journey and Entry Points](user-journey-entry-points.md) to trace these entry points through evaluation, purchase, first successful use, support and retention.

Examples:

- Google Search;
- Reddit;
- YouTube;
- Telegram;
- GitHub;
- recommendations;
- comparison websites.

Observe:

- how users are introduced to the product;
- whether trust is established before registration.

## Evidence required for conclusions

- Official channel lists establish presence, not reach or preference.
- Content availability does not establish that users read it or that it changes trust.
- Community activity should be measured before describing a channel as important.
- Open source, audits and documentation are observable trust signals; their effect on purchase decisions requires user or funnel evidence.
- Anonymous or low-friction support may align with privacy positioning, but its operational and user value should be tested.
