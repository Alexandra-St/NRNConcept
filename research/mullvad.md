# Competitor: Mullvad

## Purpose

Assess how Mullvad turns privacy principles into visible account, pricing and data-handling decisions.

## Evidence status

Review date: 18 July 2026.

Product and policy statements are based on Mullvad's official documentation. Communication conclusions are interpretations. This review does not independently validate security performance or user preference.

## Verified facts and company claims

Mullvad sells a VPN service using randomly generated numbered accounts. Its documentation states that signup does not require a username, password or email address.

Mullvad publishes a flat direct price of EUR 5 per month regardless of purchase duration. It supports several payment methods, including cash and cryptocurrencies, while noting that some payment methods involve processing personal data.

Mullvad states that it does not keep activity logs and describes the limited account, payment and support data it may process. These are company claims documented in its policies, not a substitute for independent verification.

## Communication interpretation

The account model, flat pricing and detailed data-handling policy make the privacy position visible in the product flow. The tone is restrained and specific; limitations and exceptions are usually stated near the relevant claim.

## Strengths observed in the communication

- A simple product and pricing model.
- Privacy claims are connected to concrete account and data-retention decisions.
- Policies explain exceptions instead of implying that no data is ever processed.
- The service avoids requiring an email address at signup.

## Limitations of this review

- The review did not test the apps or support experience.
- It does not establish whether beginners find the product easy to understand.
- The no-logging statement is a company claim; audit evidence should be reviewed separately for technical assurance.

## Implications for Narayana

**Interpretation:** visible data-minimization choices can communicate privacy more credibly than broad slogans.

**Hypothesis to test:** explaining exactly which data each Narayana service needs, and why, will increase trust without reducing purchase completion.
