# Communication Channels Audit

## Goal

Verify where Narayana and the selected privacy-focused competitors communicate with users, provide support, maintain communities and deliver their products.

Related research: [Business Messaging Ecosystem](business-messaging-ecosystem.md) describes how Telegram and WhatsApp are used as business interfaces beyond the reviewed competitor set.

## Scope and method

Companies reviewed:

- Narayana;
- Proton;
- Mullvad;
- Signal;
- Session;
- SimpleX;
- Silent.link;
- IVPN.

Audit date: 16 July 2026.

The audit uses official websites, support centers, documentation and official profiles linked from those properties. `Not listed` means that a channel was not presented in the reviewed official sources. It does not prove that no account or unofficial community exists.

Discovery sources and the geographic distribution of users cannot be established from official channel lists alone.

## Verified company notes

### Narayana

The official contacts page links to the `@narayanaim` Telegram channel, X, a developer property and a Mastodon instance. The Telegram preview identifies it as **Narayana Services** and showed 213 subscribers on the audit date.

The public support options are:

- `support@narayana.im`;
- phone `+372 4444 000`;
- internal number `*4444`;
- the Telegram channel listed in the Support area.

The public site provides registration, login, pricing, FAQ and product information. Services are purchased and managed through the Narayana account rather than through a verified messenger bot.

Sources: [Narayana contacts](https://narayana.im/about/contacts), [Narayana home](https://narayana.im/welcome), [Narayana Telegram](https://t.me/narayanaim), [Narayana FAQ](https://narayana.im/about/faq).

### Proton

Proton maintains the broadest verified external communication ecosystem in the sample. Its official community page links Telegram, Discord, Matrix, Reddit, GitHub, YouTube, X, Mastodon and other social platforms.

Support is separated from community activity. Proton uses a support center and Zendesk-based contact flow. In 2026 it launched a Zendesk AI assistant that answers from support documentation, cites relevant articles and can escalate to a human advisor. Email remains an alternative.

Products and paid plans remain on Proton's website and apps; Telegram is not presented as a purchase or account-management interface.

Sources: [Proton community](https://proton.me/community), [Proton support](https://proton.me/support), [Proton's Zendesk and AI support explanation](https://proton.me/support/zendesk).

### Mullvad

Mullvad's official site links X, Mastodon, YouTube and GitHub. Help is provided through guides, FAQ and email. Users can also report a problem from the Mullvad app without providing an email address, although an email is required to receive a reply.

Telegram, WhatsApp and a user-facing bot or AI assistant are not listed on the reviewed official pages.

Sources: [Mullvad website](https://mullvad.net/en), [Mullvad bug and support reporting](https://mullvad.net/en/help/how-report-bug-or-vulnerability).

### Signal

Signal links its Community forum, GitHub, Bluesky, Instagram, Mastodon and X. Support is initiated from the app or an official web form; legitimate replies use an `@signal.org` email address.

Signal has a one-way official chat inside the Signal app for product announcements. Signal explicitly states that this chat is not customer support and warns that accounts claiming to be support bots or representatives inside Signal are scams.

Sources: [Signal website](https://signal.org/), [Signal support contact methods](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/6746004451610-Contact-Us), [Signal Official Chat](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/10933746286746-Signal-Official-Chat).

### Session

Session directs users to its own app and Session Community. Its FAQ identifies three support paths:

- Session's Zendesk support portal;
- Session Community inside the app;
- GitHub issues for technical bug reports.

Session's Terms mention communities on Session and Telegram as update channels, but the reviewed official materials do not identify a Telegram service bot or a purchase flow inside Telegram.

Sources: [Session website](https://getsession.org/), [Session FAQ](https://getsession.org/faq), [Session documentation](https://docs.getsession.org/), [Session Terms](https://getsession.org/terms-of-service).

### SimpleX

SimpleX makes its own product the primary interaction environment. Users can contact the developers directly inside SimpleX and join official user and developer groups. Updates are distributed through the SimpleX team profile, Reddit, X, Lemmy, Mastodon, Nostr, the blog, RSS and an occasional mailing list.

SimpleX publishes a bot API, SDKs and bot examples for developers. This is platform capability, not evidence of an official user-facing support or purchasing assistant.

Sources: [SimpleX website and documentation](https://simplex.chat/), [SimpleX source repository and community instructions](https://git.simplex.chat/simplex-chat/simplex-chat).

### Silent.link

Silent.link keeps the commercial flow on its website: users select a plan, pay, receive an eSIM QR code and later top up or renew through web pages. The official site links X and Matrix and provides email support.

Telegram, WhatsApp and a user-facing bot or AI assistant are not listed on the reviewed official site.

Source: [Silent.link website](https://silent.link/).

### IVPN

IVPN links Reddit, X, GitHub and Mastodon. Its site contains help content, privacy guides, a blog, transparency materials and a support contact path. Account generation and purchase happen on the website, while the VPN is used through IVPN apps.

Telegram, WhatsApp and a user-facing bot or AI assistant are not listed on the reviewed official pages.

Sources: [IVPN website](https://www.ivpn.net/en/), [IVPN ethics and communication practices](https://www.ivpn.net/en/ethics/).

## Answers to the original research questions

### Do Narayana users prefer Telegram or WhatsApp?

Public data does not answer this question. An official Telegram channel confirms that Narayana uses Telegram, but channel presence and subscriber count do not reveal what proportion of customers use Telegram or WhatsApp.

The following internal data would be required:

- anonymized acquisition source;
- country and language distribution;
- support-channel usage;
- survey responses about intended use;
- messenger-related product requests;
- conversion and support outcomes by entry channel.

### In which countries is Narayana more popular?

No reliable public customer-geography data was found during this audit. Countries where Narayana offers numbers or connectivity describe service coverage, not customer location or popularity.

### Do competitors use Telegram or WhatsApp service bots?

No verified Telegram or WhatsApp bot was found acting as the main purchase, account-management or support interface for the reviewed companies.

The closest adjacent patterns are:

- Proton's AI assistant inside its support flow;
- Signal's one-way official announcement chat inside Signal;
- Session and SimpleX communities inside their own products;
- SimpleX's bot-development platform;
- Narayana's official Telegram information channel.

These are different from a messenger bot that sells and manages telecom services.

---

## Practical conclusions

- Websites and first-party apps remain the main product and purchase interfaces across the sample.
- Help centers, documentation, email and ticket forms remain the dominant support model.
- In the reviewed sample, community channels are usually separated from account management and purchasing.
- Telegram is used by Narayana and Proton and is acknowledged by Session, but no reviewed company demonstrates a verified end-to-end telecom purchase and management flow through a Telegram bot.
- WhatsApp is not listed as an official product, support or community channel by the reviewed companies.
- Proton is the only reviewed company for which an official AI support assistant was verified.
- A Narayana messenger assistant would be a product hypothesis, not an established competitor pattern.
- Selecting Telegram or WhatsApp for such an assistant requires Narayana's internal user and acquisition data.
