# Competitor Research

## Goal

Understand how privacy-focused companies explain value, build trust, and turn technical security into understandable user benefits.

This is a communication review, not an independent security assessment, audience study or usability benchmark. Apply the evidence labels in the [Research Library](README.md).

We are not researching competitors to copy features.

We are researching them to answer:

- How do privacy products explain why they matter?
- What language do they use?
- How do they build trust?
- Which user pains do they focus on?
- Where does Narayana have a stronger opportunity?

Use the [Competitor Communication Ecosystem](competitor-communication-ecosystem.md) framework to examine discovery, communication channels, community, support, education, developer relations, trust and onboarding consistently across competitors.

## Standard profile structure

- purpose;
- evidence status;
- verified facts and company claims;
- communication interpretation;
- strengths observed in the communication;
- limitations of the review;
- implications and hypotheses for Narayana;
- sources.

---

## Evidence rules

- An official site can verify a product, policy or positioning claim, but not the objective quality of the product.
- Do not infer audience composition from website language.
- Do not use superlatives or comparative security claims without independent comparative evidence.
- Label Narayana recommendations as interpretations or hypotheses.
