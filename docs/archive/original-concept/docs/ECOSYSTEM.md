# Ecosystem

## Goal

Help ordinary people understand digital privacy and confidently choose solutions that fit their lives.

---

## Products

### Telegram Mini App

Purpose:
Client access and lightweight product interaction inside Telegram.

Role:
Standalone Telegram-native product surface and replacement for a bot.

It includes:

- Client Area
- Store
- Account
- Help
- Learn

The Mini App can exist independently inside Telegram. Its Learn section can guide users into the Privacy Lab.

---

### Privacy Lab

Purpose:
Education.

Role:
Main educational foundation connected from Learn.

It provides:

- topic discovery;
- real-life explanations;
- practical actions;
- honest, contextual product relationships;
- transitions into Interactive Simulations and Solution Finder when those modules are available.

Privacy Lab does not contain the implementation of Simulations or Solution Finder. They are separate connected modules in the ecosystem.

---

### Interactive Simulations

Purpose:
Learning through experience.

Role:
Let users explore decisions and consequences instead of only reading explanations.

---

### Solution Finder

Purpose:
Help users choose the right Narayana solution.

Role:
Continue the educational journey after the user understands the problem. Recommendations must explain both relevance and limitations.
